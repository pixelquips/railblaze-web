Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Railblaze ("the App") is a transit app for New York City. This policy explains what information the App uses, what reaches Railblaze's servers, and how you can delete it. The short version: you can use Railblaze without an account, your live location never leaves your device, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Using Railblaze Without an Account

Arrivals, maps, trip details, service alerts and Live Activities all work without signing in. In that case Railblaze keeps no personal information about you on its servers. The only things that reach our servers are the requests needed to show you transit information, described under "Live Activities and Notifications" and "Transit Data" below.

Your Location

With your permission, Railblaze uses your device's location to show nearby stations, estimate walking times and award station stamps.

  • Your live location is processed on your device and is never sent to Railblaze's servers.
  • Walking times and address search use Apple's map services, which handle those requests under Apple's privacy policy.
  • If you earn stamps while signed in, they are stored with your account (see below).

You can turn off location access at any time in your device's Settings.

Accounts (Sign in with Apple)

Favorites, stamps, service alert subscriptions and syncing between your devices require an optional account. Railblaze uses Sign in with Apple only. When you sign in, Railblaze receives and stores:

  • A Railblaze account identifier and the identifier Apple provides for your account
  • Your email address, or the private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"
  • Your name, if you choose to share it
  • When your account was created and last used

Railblaze never receives your Apple ID password.

Data Synced to Your Account

While you're signed in, the following is stored on Railblaze's servers so it stays in sync across your devices:

  • Favorites: the stations, stops, lines and directions you save
  • Service alert subscriptions: the lines you want alerts for
  • Commutes: the morning and evening trips you set up, including station, line, direction, time and days
  • Stamps and trophies: the stations you've stamped, with when you first and last stamped each one and how many times. Stamps are earned by visiting a station, so this is a record of stations you have been to.
  • Device name: the name your device reports (on current versions of iOS this is usually just "iPhone"), used to tell your devices apart

If you use Railblaze on Apple Watch, your favorites are sent from your iPhone to your watch over Apple's watch connection. They are not sent to Railblaze's servers again for this.

Live Activities and Notifications

To update a Live Activity or send you a notification, Railblaze's server needs your device's push token (a random identifier Apple assigns for delivering notifications) and the details of what to update. This happens whether or not you're signed in.

  • Tracking a train or bus: the server receives the push token, the station, line, direction and trip, and the time window you're tracking. Records of finished Live Activities are deleted after 7 days.
  • Commute Live Activities: if you turn on automatic commute tracking, the server stores your commute schedule with your device's push tokens so it can start the Live Activity at the right time.
  • Service alerts: if you're signed in, your device's push token is linked to your account so alerts for the lines you follow reach all of your devices.

Notifications are delivered through Apple's Push Notification service. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device's Settings.

Transit Data

To show arrivals and service information, the App requests data for specific stations, lines and trips from Railblaze's servers, from the MTA's public data feeds, and from Citi Bike's public station feed. These requests contain station and line identifiers, not your location or account. Like any internet request, they include your device's IP address, which the receiving service can see.

What Railblaze Does Not Do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting services
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information with third parties for their own use

Railblaze does analyze public MTA data on its servers to measure how reliable train arrival predictions are. That analysis uses no information about you.

Service Providers

Railblaze's servers run on rented cloud infrastructure, and Apple provides sign-in, notifications and map services. These providers process data only to provide those services.

How Long Data Is Kept

  • Account and synced data: until you delete your account
  • Finished Live Activity records: 7 days
  • Commute schedules: until you turn commute tracking off or delete your account

Our servers may also keep short-lived technical logs, which can include IP addresses, to keep the service running and secure.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account at any time in the App under Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and everything synced to it: favorites, alert subscriptions, commutes, stamps and trophies, and your devices' links to the account. It also revokes Railblaze's access to your Apple ID. A Live Activity that is running at that moment is allowed to finish, then deleted with other finished Live Activities.

You can also stop Railblaze's access from your Apple account settings (Sign in with Apple), or contact us to request access to or deletion of your data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and account requests require a sign-in token stored in your device's Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but Railblaze keeps as little information as it can.

Children's Privacy

Railblaze is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the App changes. Updates will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
